**R.A.T.S. Keel 0.1.4 – Euromod now runs under Keel** ⚓
Thanks to everyone who reported problems with Euromod and its sonar systems. Tested on the 3D globe beta (261107) with Euromod's Main and Expansion Packs: all 25 mods started and a mission ran. On 0.8.7 it passed the offline checks only so far.

• **Big Anchor Chain packs start properly**: every DLL loads first, then mods start in dependency order
• **Sonar Audio Loader** can search other mods' folders again
• **More mods recognised**, and anything left out is listed with the reason
• **ACConfig files** are now handled by Keel itself
• **Better version checks**: Harmony patches and by-name lookups aimed at things your build no longer has
• **New: Keel reads a mod's `_info.ini` too** and warns if its version statement doesn't fit your game

**With Euromod on the current beta**, a few features switch themselves off because the game changed (parts of EnhancedDecoyMod, MissileControlMod, Optimal Target Distance, Test Range). The game keeps running; that's for the Euromod team to update, and Keel's report names them.
