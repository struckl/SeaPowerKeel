0.5.0: Keel takes over, What's new, Euromod stages, better reports.
Keel is the loader while it is on: Anchor Chain's preloader and Alpine's Mod Loader are kept from starting, so every mod starts once, under Keel. Their files stay as they are; switch Keel off and they start again. The first start after this update still lets them run once and asks you to restart.
What's new since your last game: when mods changed, a card grows out of the Keel card at the bottom left of the main menu with the mods that are new, updated or broken and a line from their authors. A click shows everything in Keel's report.
The Keel card is now drawn by the game and stays on the main menu; both cards always fit the menu panel above the version text.
Euromod players: the report names your stage (Main Pack, + AC Pack, + Extended Guidance), a button in the report switches Extended Guidance, and an outdated AC Pack is flagged.
Mods that add menu entries at the end of the load screen (ALTERNATIVE MODS) show them on every start again.
Send report: the report says the game build and whether the game crashed; the report stays current during a Mods menu reload.
The game's message boxes say R.A.T.S. Keel in their top bar when Keel adds to them.
Run in the game on 0.9.1 (build 261112): 17 and 43 mods, Euromod with AC Pack and Extended Guidance in a mission, Alpine kept out, Mods menu reload and restart.
