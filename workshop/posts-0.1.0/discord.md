**R.A.T.S. Keel – faster, safer modding on every game version** ⚓
Anchor Chain got Sea Power's code mods going – I built Keel to take it further. It runs your Anchor Chain mods as they are, and adds checks, a report, better logs and globe-beta support. Use it instead of Anchor Chain's loader, or keep both side by side while you try it.
**Updated regularly** – follows every game update and beta build, updates arrive through the Workshop.

• **Your mods on the 3D globe beta – today** (Anchor Chain's current release doesn't start there yet; tested on build 261107 + 0.8.7)
• **Outdated mods can't break your missions anymore** – every mod is checked against your game version first
• **Faster start** – mods ready at 37 s instead of 116 s
• **One broken mod can't freeze the game**
• **Only the mods you ticked load**
• **Right version of a mod picked for your game** automatically
• **Ctrl+Shift+K** shows what runs and why something doesn't
• **Timestamped logs** naming the mod behind each error

**Install:** subscribe → copy `SeaPowerKeel.Stub.dll` (folder `copy-to-BepInEx-plugins` in Keel's Workshop folder) into `BepInEx\plugins` → to let Keel do all the loading, remove `AnchorChain.Preloader.dll` (keep Anchor Chain subscribed, your mods need its files).

Thanks to the Anchor Chain team – Keel stands on their work.
