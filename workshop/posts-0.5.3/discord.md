**R.A.T.S. Keel 0.5 – drop the anchor, raise the keel** ⚓
Keel keeps your modded game afloat. Biggest Keel update so far:

**What's new since your last game – right on the main menu**
• A card splits off the Keel card: mods that are new, updated, gone or broken
• The author's own notes, one line each; click a mod for all of it
• Right click and both cards get out of your way

**One loader, every mod once**
• Keel takes over from Anchor Chain and Alpine's loader: no mod starts twice
• Nothing to uninstall; switch Keel off and they start again
• Your Anchor Chain mods and BepInEx plugins run as they are

**Know which mod broke – and why**
• Every mod checked against your game version before it starts
• A broken mod is marked, the rest sails on
• Report on Ctrl+Shift+K, Send report in one click

**Also**
• The game's "not compatible" question: short names, code vs data mods, made-for version
• Euromod: report names your stage, Extended Guidance on/off with one button
• Loading window top right, right click hides the cards, works with Alternative Game Interface
