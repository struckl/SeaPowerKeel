# First run in the game – 2026-11-07, Sea Power 0.8.7 (flat map, build 261021)

Mods switched on: BetterTacMap, Anchor Chain (loader), IdentifyExpanded, Radio, Topographic Map, Cities and Borders, plus a
temporary `KeelTest` folder with test plugins (fine, dependent on fine ≥ 1.1, needs fine ≥ 2.0, missing dependency, throws
in `Awake`, throws from `Update` after 20 s, starts after the thrower, and a DLL built against the globe build with a field
of a globe-only type).

## Run A – next to Anchor Chain (its 2024 preloader in BepInEx\plugins)

- Keel left all 8 Anchor Chain plugins to Anchor Chain ("left to Anchor Chain"), nothing started twice.
- Test plugins: fine and dependent ran (dependent saw fine in `Chainloader.PluginInfos`), the thrower was marked FAILED and
  the next plugin still started, "needs 2.0", "missing dependency" and the globe-only DLL were refused with their reasons.
- Seen on the way: Anchor Chain 1.1.0 loads every DLL of every mod folder, also folders switched off in the Mods menu (here
  Pop-Out, Sensor Views, Automatic SAR, Dynamic Campaign Mod, rgreat's three …) and the globe-only test DLL. Its preloader
  also holds up BepInEx's start-up (+28 s to +116 s here), so Keel only got going after it.
- Bugs found and fixed: blame on the wrong plugin of a DLL, empty stub version in the log, notice gone before the menu,
  report window unreadably small on 4K.

## Run B – Anchor Chain's preloader removed, Keel runs everything

- Keel read the game's mod list at +31.9 s, found 16 plugins in 10 DLLs (1.1 s), checked them (0.4 s) and had all 13 that
  may run started at +37.2 s (Anchor Chain needed until +116 s in run A). Only switched-on folders.
- All 8 Anchor Chain mods ran under Keel (types from Anchor Chain's Workshop DLL); our mods' gates picked the flat builds.
- Intro 01 loaded; Topographic Map and Cities and Borders attached to the map and drew (screenshot), no exceptions.
- Notice in the corner at the main menu, readable on 4K: "5 mod(s) did not start or threw errors … Ctrl+Shift+K".
